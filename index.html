🎮 Game development for beginners

Level Up: Making Your First Game

Game development is the craft of turning an idea into something people can play. It mixes art, code, sound and design, but you don't need to master all of them to begin. Start with something tiny, finish it, and every game after that gets easier.

How a Game Gets Made

Most games, big or small, go through the same five stages.

  1. 💡

    Idea

    Pick one simple thing the player does, like jump, match or dodge, and build around it.

  2. 🧪

    Prototype

    Make a rough, ugly version fast to find out whether the idea is actually fun.

  3. 🎨

    Art & Sound

    Add visuals, music and sound effects that make the game feel alive.

  4. 🕹️

    Playtest

    Watch other people play without helping them, then fix what confuses them.

  5. 🚀

    Release

    Share it with friends or online. A finished small game beats an unfinished big one.

Small projects that teach the basics. Use the buttons to filter by type.

    Two paddles, one ball. A classic first project for learning movement, collisions and scoring.

    • 2D
  • 🗺️

    Text Adventure

    One-Screen Platformer

    Run, jump and reach the flag. Good practice for gravity, controls and level design.

    • 2D
